# Orbit test checklist

Tick each one off, and note anything odd. "Say" means during a conversation (tap the eyes,
or say "Orby").

## Eyes
- [ ] Opening the app: black from the first moment (no white screen), then the intro fades in
- [ ] Start-up: moon orbits the planet, then both slide smoothly into the eyes (no jump)
- [ ] Say "make a happy face": rings open at the bottom into smiling arches, then close again
- [ ] Move side to side and up/down: eyes follow further than before, never clipped at an edge
- [ ] No dark bar anywhere with scan lines on (try a few line counts on the Eyes tab)
- [ ] Double-tap: eyes close into gently upward-arched slits (not flat ovals), breathe slowly, little z's float up
- [ ] Asleep with you still there: the closed eyes (and the head, with a neck) turn slowly and sleepily toward you; the head nods gently with each breath
- [ ] Double-tap again: wakes

## Talking
- [ ] Say "what am I holding?" (hold something up): eyes widen, it describes it
- [ ] Say "what's on my desk?": eyes look down; without the neck it says it can't tilt yet
- [ ] Say "remind me in 1 minute to stretch": it confirms the time; a minute later it chimes,
      looks surprised and tells you (try once mid-conversation, once after it has ended)
- [ ] Say "set a 2 minute timer", then "cancel the timer": it cancels
- [ ] Reminders show on the Orbit page with Cancel buttons
- [ ] Double-tap to sleep, set a 1-minute reminder first: it wakes up for it
- [ ] Say "louder" / "quieter"
- [ ] Say "goodnight": says goodnight, then sleeps
- [ ] Say "that's all": says bye, stops listening
- [ ] Say "don't say hi when I come back", then check the greeting switch is off (turn it back on)

## Its own life
- [ ] Orbit page → Its own life → Do homework now: a hobby and a journal entry appear
- [ ] Say "what have you been up to?": it mentions its hobby
- [ ] Next time it greets you after homework: a quick hello plus one line about what it learned
- [ ] Say "you should take up astronomy": the hobby changes (Orbit page → Its own life)

## Arriving
- [ ] Leave for 10+ minutes, come back: happy eyes, quick hello
- [ ] (Optional) Privacy → Fine-tune → "Notice things when I arrive" on: next arrival it may
      mention something it sees (once a day)

## Device → Neck (no hardware needed)
- [ ] Moves box: the dot follows you as you move; buttons nod / shake / droop etc.
- [ ] Small moves in your chair: only the eyes follow, the head (dot) stays still. Lean well to one side: after a moment the head turns once, smoothly, and settles
- [ ] "How often the head turns": Often / Natural / Rarely change how far you can move first
- [ ] "Show the neck's angles on the eyes": small text appears in the top corner (then turn off)

## Sounds and habits (Orbit page → Sounds and habits)
- [ ] Start-up: a soft whoosh with the orbit animation
- [ ] Preview buttons play each sound; volume slider works; Sounds off silences them
- [ ] Tap to talk: a small rising tone; conversation ends: a falling tone
- [ ] Double-tap: a sigh as it falls asleep; double-tap again: a chirp as it wakes
- [ ] Leave it until it dozes: it yawns (eyes squeeze, little yawn sound)
- [ ] Clap once loudly while it's idle: it jumps (and wakes if dozing)
- [ ] Nobody in view, wave a hand at the side of the camera: the eyes glance toward it
- [ ] While it watches you, the eyes make tiny flicks (subtle)
- [ ] Come back after 10+ min: glance away, double-take, then happy eyes
- [ ] "Set a 3 minute timer": a thin ring appears around the right eye and drains;
      when done, the rings pulse and a rising chime plays
- [ ] Weather in the eyes: enter your town → Show now → Back: tint + rain/snow/etc.
- [ ] Replay its first wake-up: eyes flutter open, look around, then it introduces itself
- [ ] (Later) Gentle nudges: after 2 h at the desk it suggests a stretch; after 11 pm, bed

## Alive, round 2
- [ ] Orbit → Sounds and habits → "Try a habit" buttons: each plays on the eyes (slow blink, content, wonder,
      ponder, sneeze, hiccup, stretch, firefly, roll eyes, peek, shiver)
- [ ] Firefly: a little glowing dot drifts around; the eyes follow it
- [ ] Leave it idle with you there for a few minutes: little moves happen by themselves
- [ ] Talk near it (not to it): the eyes perk up and tilt a little
- [ ] Ask "show me you're content" / "ponder that": it uses the new expressions

## Sleep
- [ ] Double-tap to sleep while sitting there: it stays asleep. Leave for 30 s, come back: it wakes
- [ ] Say "privacy mode": camera and mic off ("camera and microphone off" hint); coming back does
      NOT wake it; double-tap does
- [ ] Sound previews: every sound is soft; no "slap" (especially Startle, Sneeze, Hiccup)

## Looking at your screen
- [ ] (Neck needed) Say "Orby, find my screen" (or Device → Neck → Find my screen): it looks around for
      ~30 s, turns to the screen, asks if that's right; "a bit left" / "up" / "perfect" work
- [ ] (Optional) "Or aim by hand" still works
- [ ] Ask "what do you think of what's on my screen?" → the head turns to the screen, then back;
      it describes what's there
- [ ] Without a neck: it says it can't turn to the screen yet

## Orbit Desk (optional)
- [ ] On the PC: run_orbit_desk.bat → shows name, address, pairing code
- [ ] Device → Looking at your computer screen → Find my computer → pick it → code → Test → "✓ Connected to …"
- [ ] Device → Looking at your computer screen → "Optional: sharper text with Orbit Desk" on → ask about your screen → eyes
      glance toward the computer, it describes the screen; the PC window lists the look
- [ ] Type p + Enter on the PC, ask again → it says sharing is paused
- [ ] Eyes tab → Copy my look → change something → Paste a look → it goes back

## Premium round
- [ ] Say "hi Orby, how are you?" mid-conversation: it doesn't think you said "a or b"
- [ ] Wake with "Hey Orbit" too
- [ ] Smile at it: it smiles back
- [ ] Stroke a finger across its eyes: happy squint and a soft purr
- [ ] Hand gestures (on by default, downloads once): wave, 👍, 👎, ✌, 🤟; a finger up while it talks → it stops
      and goes quiet (red screen edge); a tap, a wave or "Orby" ends the quiet
- [ ] Memory → Know who's who on → type your name → Learn face (look at it) → leave 10+ min and come back:
      it greets you by name. Or say "Orby, this is Mike"
- [ ] Conversation → My calendar on (allow) → next morning hello mentions your day; 10 min before an event a ring
- [ ] "Help me focus for 2 minutes": calm eyes, ring drains, celebration + chime at the end
- [ ] "What did we do this week?" after a few days
- [ ] Play music near it (not talking): eyes bob to the beat after a few seconds
- [ ] Set your birthday to today (Privacy → See what Orbit remembers): confetti on start-up, birthday wish on next hello
- [ ] Turn off Wi-Fi: a crossed-out Wi-Fi symbol appears top-right and the hint says "Offline · no internet"
- [ ] Screen edge glows (not the eyes): a timer → green; a reminder → amber; "help me focus" → violet;
      a "shh" finger or "privacy mode" → red. The countdown ring around the eye stays soft cyan
- [ ] Talk to it: the eyes don't open at the bottom. Stay quiet and smile for a second: happy ^ ^ arches

## Settings and voices
- [ ] Long-press: every section is listed down the left, nothing hidden; Back is top right
- [ ] Conversation → Voice: 10 voices in three groups (Female, Male, Neutral)
- [ ] Media volume up. Tap a voice → a short pulse, then it says hello in that voice (bars dance);
      tap another mid-sentence → the first stops; tap a heard one again → plays instantly
- [ ] Rows of choices wrap onto a new line instead of running off the screen

## Eyes, quicker
- [ ] "Wink at me": a quick, cheeky wink (under a second)
- [ ] Smile at it / thumbs up: the eyes pop into the happy arch quickly, no slow "draining"
- [ ] Eyes move smoothly when following you (no stepping); when you move, they land with a tiny overshoot
- [ ] Watch closely: the two eyes blink very slightly apart
- [ ] When it smiles, a quick happy hop/wiggle as the arch arrives

## Premium polish
- [ ] Fresh install (or clear Orbit's storage): after the first wake-up, the welcome setup appears;
      "Skip setup" (top right) works on every page; Back/Next work; the dots show progress
- [ ] Welcome: add the key, pick a name and voice (hear it), your name, appliance switches; "Say hello" → it talks
- [ ] Settings → Device: "Start when the phone turns on" → asks for "Display over other apps" → restart the
      phone → Orbit opens by itself (unlock first if you have a PIN)
- [ ] "Lock to Orbit": Home and Back don't leave; hold Back + Recents to get out
- [ ] Tap to talk: the eyes perk up and the listening tone plays straight away, before it's connected
- [ ] While it thinks (or connects), a small comet spins in the top-right corner
- [ ] While it talks, the eyes lift a little with each syllable
- [ ] Sounds: start-up, waking, listening, ending, reminder and timer all share the same three notes
- [ ] Turn Wi-Fi off mid-conversation: plain message ("I lost the connection to Google" or offline), no error codes
- [ ] Launch kit: README shows the header image

## Music
- [ ] Settings → Music → Allow → turn on Orbit in Android's list → back: "✓ Orbit can control your music apps"
- [ ] Music app: pick Spotify (or YouTube Music); open that app once
- [ ] "Try: play something chill" plays; Pause / Next work
- [ ] Talk: "play some jazz", "skip", "who's this?", "louder", "stop the music in 2 minutes" (fades out, eyes dim)
- [ ] While music plays: the song's name fades in; the eyes take the album's colour; they bob (and sway with the neck)
- [ ] Quick quiet, each one stops the music: tap Orbit · hold up a hand ✋ · finger to lips 🤫 · cover the camera
- [ ] Tap Orbit to talk while music plays → the music dips, then comes back when the conversation ends
- [ ] Swipe across the eyes right-to-left → next song; left-to-right → previous; 👍 → "Noted: you like this one"
- [ ] A slow song: eyes half-close and smile a little
- [ ] After a few days: "help me focus for 25 minutes" → it offers what you usually focus to

## Welcome eyes
- [ ] Welcome setup / Eyes preview: the eyes glance about naturally (short looks, sometimes at you), blink, no metronome swing

## Settings, simpler (5 pages)
- [ ] Long-press: five pages down the left: Orbit, Eyes, Music, Privacy, Device (no key yet → it opens on Device)
- [ ] Each page shows a few main things; "Show ▾" groups open the rest
- [ ] Orbit → Liveliness: all the way left = no little habits; right = they come more often
- [ ] Eyes → Looks: Holographic / Classic / Neon / Minimal / Retro change the preview at once; Customize has every slider
- [ ] Privacy → Private / Balanced / Social: the switches under Fine-tune follow; changing one shows "Custom"
- [ ] Device → Status card is right (key, wake word, music, neck, temperature)

## Radio and soundscapes
- [ ] "Play some jazz" / "something to focus to" / "lofi": a station starts within a few seconds, its name fades in
- [ ] "Next" → another station; "pause" / tap Orbit → stops; "resume" → back
- [ ] "Play rain" / "ocean" / "fire" / "brown noise" / "ambient": plays with the internet off too, fades in gently
- [ ] Talking to Orbit while they play: they dip, then come back
- [ ] "Stop the music in 1 minute": it fades out and stops
- [ ] Music → "Link music accounts" (open by itself until linked): 3 simple steps; tapping a service opens it (or its Play Store page); installed ones show ✓; the button → Android's list → back: "✓ Done"; the heading then shows "Music accounts · ✓ Spotify"
- [ ] Without any music app: "play Taylor Swift" plays a radio station instead and Orbit says so

## Music and talking
- [ ] With music playing, start saying "Hey Orby": the music drops right down before you finish; it comes back after the conversation
- [ ] Say something with "or" in it (not its name): the music dips briefly, back within 3 seconds
- [ ] "Play some jazz": Orbit says a couple of words or nothing, no "enjoy the music, Mike"
- [ ] Over a conversation, it uses your name only now and then

## Weather
- [ ] First time: "What's the weather?" → it asks your town; say it → "saved" → it answers
- [ ] Ask again another day: no question, just the answer (it remembered)
- [ ] It says the weather ONCE: how it is now, then the high and low with roughly when ("high of 64 around 3 PM, low of 48 around 6 AM")
- [ ] As it answers, the eyes glide smoothly to the middle, grow and come close together: the rings spill past the screen edges; a coloured animated icon in the left eye (gold sun, moon, cloud, fog, blue rain, snow, storm with a yellow bolt), the temperature in the right (big white number, under it the high in red and the low in blue, bold arrows)
- [ ] The zoom stays while it talks and glides back out about 2 seconds after it stops; no blinks while zoomed
- [ ] Ask the same question again straight away: it doesn't repeat the whole forecast
- [ ] "What's the weather tomorrow?" / "What about Saturday?" / "This weekend?": it talks only about that day, and the eyes show that day: its icon, the day's name, big red ↑high and blue ↓low (no current temperature)
- [ ] "What's the week looking like?": a short overall picture; eyes show THIS WEEK with the week's high and low
- [ ] "Weather in 10 days?": it says the forecast doesn't go that far
- [ ] °F and °C both fit inside the ring (try a 3-digit temperature if you can, e.g. a hot town)
- [ ] "Will it rain this afternoon?" / "What about tomorrow?" / "Do I need a coat?" → short, sensible answers
- [ ] Orbit → Weather → °C / °F: answers switch units
- [ ] Next morning's first hello mentions the weather in one sentence (switch: "Mention it in the morning")
- [ ] "I've moved to Denver": it saves the new town

## Calm eyes
- [ ] Leave it with nobody around: after a few minutes it dozes with closed, arched eyes (not ovals), drifting slowly
- [ ] Double-tap: asleep, the same arched eyes, breathing, z's
- [ ] Sit still in front of it: the eyes rest on you calmly, only tiny occasional flicks
- [ ] Nobody there and nothing moving: an unhurried look somewhere every 6–14 seconds, often back to the middle

## Getting sleepy
- [ ] Doze after = 1 min, step away: about 40 s before, the lids get heavy, slow blinks, a yawn; then the eyes close slowly into arcs (dozing, no z's)
- [ ] Closing never looks like the ring draining: the lids flatten to a line, then the arch appears
- [ ] Then sleep after = 1 min: it falls properly asleep (sigh, floating z's); walk back: it wakes
- [ ] No chime while it looks around (the firefly is silent now)
- [ ] Orbit → Sounds and habits → Try a habit: glitch, blink game, dizzy, boing (short and silly; they also happen rarely by themselves)

## Music icon

## Neck round 2
- [ ] Double-tap to sleep: the head bows in about 2 seconds, smoothly (no jerk), then breathes slowly
- [ ] Let it doze: the head lowers smoothly within a few seconds
- [ ] Move slowly to one side: the head turns once to face you and settles, no hunting back and forth
- [ ] Turn away or step out of view briefly: the head waits a few seconds (doesn't wander off), then glances the way you went
- [ ] "Look to your left" / "look right a bit" / "look up" / "look all the way to the right": it turns that way (left/right as you see it) and holds about 30 s, then comes back to you
- [ ] "Turn to YOUR left": it turns to your right as you see it
- [ ] "Look over there and tell me what you see": it turns, then takes a photo of the new view
- [ ] "Look at me": it comes straight back

## Music you can hear
- [ ] "Orby, play some jazz": a couple of words from Orbit, the conversation closes by itself, and the music comes up to full volume over about a second
- [ ] Phone media volume at zero, then "play some music": it plays anyway (volume raised to 40 %)
- [ ] "Orby" while music plays: it drops right down; ask something; after the conversation it comes back up smoothly

## Conversation diary
- [ ] Have a real chat (a plan, a problem, something you did). After it ends, Privacy → See what Orbit remembers shows a one-line diary entry about it within a few seconds
- [ ] "Play some jazz" or "what's the weather?" alone: no diary entry (skipped)
- [ ] Next conversation (or next day): Orbit can pick up the thread ("how did the neck go?") and answers "what were we talking about yesterday?"
- [ ] "What did we talk about last month?" uses search_diary for older entries
- [ ] Switch "Remember our conversations" off: no new entries

## Messages for people

- [ ] With Know who's who on and Sarah's face learned: when Orbit sees her, it greets her and gives the message
- [ ] Without her face: Sarah says "it's Sarah, any messages for me?" and gets it
- [ ] Next time you talk to Orbit, it tells you Sarah got your message (and roughly when)

## Can it see me / face me
- [ ] Step out of view and ask "can you see me?": it says no (and when it last saw you); step in: yes
- [ ] Out of view, "face me" / "look at me": the head turns the way you went, then sweeps; it says when it finds you, or asks you to step in front

## Watching like a dog
- [ ] Sit near it and move slowly around: the head keeps your face centred with small smooth moves, no jitter, no overshoot
- [ ] Stay still for a few minutes: now and then (every 1–2 min) it glances away for a couple of seconds, then back to you
- [ ] Walk across the room: it keeps a looser eye on you and looks about more often
- [ ] Step out of view: it waits a moment, looks the way you went, glances at movement, then sweeps the room every 25–45 s (Status → "Head: scanning the room for you")
- [ ] In a conversation, out of view: it sweeps more often (every ~15 s)
- [ ] Settings → Device → Neck → "How closely it watches you": Closely / Natural / Loosely change how readily it re-centres

## Soft start + hints
- [ ] Firmware 3 (by USB, or as the first Bluetooth update): Serial Monitor "firmware 3"
- [ ] Switch the neck's power off and on: no jolt; it starts where it was and drifts to the middle
- [ ] Restart the app with the neck powered: the first move is slow and soft
- [ ] After start-up the hints stand big and bold on the left (• talk / •• sleep / — settings), fading after 6 s; other messages still at the bottom
