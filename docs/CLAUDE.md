# Orbit — phone-as-face AI desk companion

## What this is

An open-source desktop AI companion. A dedicated old Android phone is the face and brain; it sits on a small pan/tilt servo neck (Yahboom 2-DOF kit + ESP32 over BLE) so it can look at you, track you, and emote. It's a friendly character and a natural way to talk to AI, not a task robot.

* Goal: working proof of concept, published as open source. Not a product.
* Budget: free/cheap to run. Paid AI tiers are a stretch goal.
* Full project brief (decisions, architecture, state machine, risks): https://claude.ai/artifact/XTLafYw4Y8FGpbjwScP7K3

**Eyes**
* Start-up: `Intro` (in Face.kt), a 1.4 s animation: a moon orbits a planet in the eye style (hidden behind the planet on the far side), then the orbit straightens and the planet/moon settle exactly into the left/right eyes' resting spots. Intro and eyes share `animationStart`, so the shimmer doesn't jump at the hand-over.
* Eye position is clamped so both eyes and their glow stay fully on screen (uses the eyes' real, perspective-shortened height). Tracking range: 0.22 × width sideways, 0.32 × height up/down (before clamping). The eye Canvas is an offscreen layer; status-bar contrast scrim off. The remaining translucent dark bar turned out to come from the scan lines: a fractional line spacing beats against the pixel grid into a wide moiré band. Fixed by rounding the spacing to whole pixels.
* Sizing uses `eyeBase()` = min(height, 0.76 × width), so the eyes still fit if Android 16 ignores landscape on large screens; settings tabs scroll sideways.
* Full-screen, landscape-locked, keep-screen-on. Two rings drawn on a Compose Canvas, 60 fps.
* Styles: **Holographic** (default: white core, drifting cyan/magenta chromatic-aberration fringes that lag when moving, soft glow, turning pastel rainbow sheen via Multiply blend, bright edge lines, gliding glint, short trail) or **Classic** (plain white-cyan ring). Every holographic effect is adjustable/switchable in settings.
* Blinks; perspective when looking sideways/up/down; tracks your face (CameraX + ML Kit, on-device); glances around when nobody is seen.
* Asleep: eyes breathe (5 s cycle: size, openness, brightness), drift slowly (burn-in), and three glowing "z"s float up from beside the right eye (`drawSleepZs`, fades in/out with `sleepZ`).
* Moods (`Mood` enum) drive the look: IDLE, LISTENING (attentive), HEARING (swell with your voice), THINKING (narrowed, glance up-right), SPEAKING (bounce with Gemini's voice), DOZING, ASLEEP.

**Voice**
* `GeminiLive`: Gemini Live API over OkHttp WebSocket, model `gemini-3.8-live`, audio replies, voice and personality from settings. **Key goes in the `x-goog-api-key` header** (the `?key=` query was rejected with 1007).
* Tap to start/end a conversation.
* Interrupting: with "Let me interrupt" on, conversations switch the phone to speakerphone-call audio (MODE_IN_COMMUNICATION, loudspeaker, USAGE_VOICE_COMMUNICATION playback) so echo cancelling works, the mic keeps streaming while Gemini talks, and `serverContent.interrupted` stops playback (generation counter drops queued audio). Off = old half-duplex behaviour. Needs MODIFY\_AUDIO\_SETTINGS.
* System instruction gets the current date/time. Optional Google Search tool. If Google refuses search + function tools together before setup completes, it reconnects without search.
* Expressions: `express` function tool (NON\_BLOCKING, answered with scheduling SILENT). Expressions: happy (the ring itself opens at the bottom into a ^ arch: `EyeShape.sweep` 360→160°, drawn with drawArc + round caps; no black mask), surprised, curious (tilt + uneven eyes), sad, nod, shake\_head, look\_away, wink; 2.5 s each.
* Greeting: when a face appears after being away ≥ N minutes (default 10), it opens a conversation and sends a text turn so Orbit speaks first: a quick casual hello of a few words, not a conversation starter. Interrupting and greeting are on by default.
* Ends by itself after a quiet spell (default 30 s) to save the free allowance.
* Context window compression (sliding window) is on, and session resumption handles are kept; on `goAway` it reconnects and carries on. (Not yet tested in a long conversation.)
* Its name (`companionName`, default "Orbit"; nickname Orby) is prepended to the instructions; personality presets (Friendly, Calm, Cheeky, Curious, Butler) in `PERSONALITIES`. Captions: setup asks for `outputAudioTranscription`; text shown at the bottom while it talks (off by default).
* Wake word (off by default): Vosk (`com.alphacephei:vosk-android:0.3.75@aar` + `net.java.dev.jna:jna:5.18.1@aar`) with a grammar of just the wake words + `"[unk]"`. Wake words setting is comma-separated, default `orbit, orby`; words missing from the model's dictionary are mapped to sound-alikes (`orby` → `or be`) in `SOUNDS_LIKE`, fed our own 16 kHz mic chunks between conversations (not while asleep). Model `vosk-model-small-en-us-0.15` (\~40 MB) is downloaded from alphacephei.com and unzipped into `filesDir/vosk` on first enable. Triggers on the partial result containing the phrase. Sound-alikes (2026-10-28, after it didn't respond): orby → or be / or bee / orb e / or b; orbit → orbit / or bit / orb it. Settings shows a live check (sound reaching it? last heard text; "✓ Heard the wake word!"); hearing it while settings are open doesn't start a conversation. On trigger: "curious" expression + start conversation; mic audio captured while connecting is buffered (≤5 s) and sent once setup completes. Chosen over openWakeWord because any phrase works without training a model.
* Voice commands (function tools, always on): `go_to_sleep` and `end_conversation` (both wait for it to finish speaking, max 8 s), `change_volume(direction)` (2 steps on voice-call and music streams), `set_greeting(on)`. Routed through `GeminiLive.onCommand` to `MainActivity.voiceCommand`. Listed on the About tab.
* Vision ("Let it see when I ask", on by default): blocking `look` tool. On call: "looking" expression (eyes widen, look ahead) + status "Looking…"; `GeminiLive.onLook` asks MainActivity for the next camera frame (`photoWanted`, 3 s timeout), which becomes an upright ≤768 px JPEG (`ImageProxy.toBitmap`, rotate, compress 80) sent as `realtimeInput.video` (image/jpeg), then the tool response. Never takes photos unprompted (except the opt-in daily "notice things"). `look(where)`: "ahead" (default) or "down" (desk). Down shows the `looking_down` expression for 4 s (eyes look down; `Neck` tilts fully down at 4/s); with a connected neck (`gemini.canLookDown`) the photo waits 1.2 s on its own thread for the head to arrive, without one Gemini is told the photo is straight ahead and to ask them to hold it up. `showExpression(name, ms)` now takes a duration.
* Reminders/timers: blocking tools `set_reminder(what, in_minutes | at "HH:MM")` (next occurrence) and `cancel_reminder(what | "all")`, answered with text so Gemini confirms the time. Stored in `Settings.reminders` ("ms|what" lines). MainActivity checks every second; when due: wakes from sleep, chime (`ToneGenerator` TONE_PROP_ACK), surprised eyes, then `gemini.tell()` mid-conversation or opens a conversation with `reminderDue(what)`; no key → status text. Pending reminders listed in the live instructions and on the Memory tab (Cancel buttons). Overdue ones fire when the app starts.
* Inner life (on by default): `Settings.hobby`, `journal` (dated, newest 30), `lastHomework` (date). Once a day while no conversation is open (checked every 30 s in MainActivity), `InnerLife.doHomework()` picks a text model from ListModels (newest `flash`, skipping image/tts/audio/live/embedding/vision/lite), calls `generateContent` with `googleSearch` (retries without search if refused), asks for JSON `{hobby, entry}`; first run picks a hobby. Hobby + last 5 journal entries go into the live instructions; `set_hobby(hobby)` tool lets the person suggest one. Memory tab shows hobby/journal (editable) + "Do homework now".
* "THINKING" is detected locally: you talked, then went quiet, and no reply audio yet.
* Memory: `Settings.memory` holds dated notes (one per line, max 80), plus `userName`. Both go into the system instruction (notes numbered). Gemini saves/edits via function tools `remember(note)`, `forget(number)` (number refers to the list sent at setup), `set_user_name(name)`; all NON\_BLOCKING/SILENT. Memory tab shows/edits/wipes them. Stored only on the phone; sent to Gemini at each conversation start.

**Power and privacy**
* Heat watchdog (every 10 s): warm (thermal MODERATE+ or battery ≥ 42°C) → eyes ×0.5 glow, face checks ≤ 2/s, no homework, idle status "too warm · resting"; hot (SEVERE+ or ≥ 46°C) → privacy sleep (camera off to cool), auto-wake when cooled (only if the heat put it to sleep). Cools only at ≤ 39°C and thermal < MODERATE (hysteresis). Temperature shown on the Tracking & night tab.
* Two kinds of sleep (2026-10-28): **sleep** (double-tap, "goodnight" / `go_to_sleep`, night schedule): mic off, conversation ended, eyes shut + breathing + z's, camera stays on checking for a face once a second (on the phone) and wakes when a face appears after none for 20 s (and ≥ 20 s after falling asleep, so it doesn't wake while you're still sitting there); neck bows gently, still facing the room. **Privacy sleep** ("privacy mode" / `privacy_mode` tool, heat): camera unbound too, wakes only on double-tap; neck turns away and down; eyes a little darker; status "••  wake · camera and microphone off". MainActivity `asleep` + `privacy`, `sleptAt`, `lastFaceAsleep`; Face `isPrivate`; `neck.privacy`.
* Dozing: after N minutes (default 3) with no face seen and no touch, eyes go dim/half-closed and drift slowly around (burn-in protection); face detection drops to twice a second (heat). A face or a tap wakes it; the first tap only wakes.
* Status text at the bottom fades out 6 s after each change (burn-in). The idle hint uses symbols: `•  talk   ••  sleep   —  settings` (tap / double-tap / long-press).
* Night schedule (off by default): between set hours, either dim the eyes or sleep (ordinary sleep, so a face still wakes it; auto-wakes at the end only if the schedule put it to sleep). Checked every 30 s.
* Full screen: system bars re-hidden on window focus and when leaving settings; nav-bar contrast scrim off; draws into the camera cutout (fix for a dark box at the bottom).
* "Hints at the bottom" switch hides the status text entirely.
* Optional CRT scan lines over the eyes (`drawScanLines`: one repeating-gradient rect; on by default; adjustable number of lines (30–400 over the screen height, default 360) and line thickness (dark share of each stripe); lines are 80% black; spacing rounded to whole pixels (fractional spacing made a moiré bar); the lit band fades softly at its edges so fat lines look rounded).

**Neck** (built 2026-10-28, hardware not yet arrived; app + firmware untested, firmware not compiled)
* `Neck.step(dt)` runs ~30×/s from a `lifecycleScope` loop in MainActivity. Face sets `neck.mood` each tick. Pose in degrees from straight ahead: + pan = the way the eyes move right on screen, + tilt = down.
* Face seen: closed loop (camera turns with the head): target = current aim + (face offset beyond a dead zone, 0.12 x / 0.15 y, of `tracker.rawX/rawY`) × half camera view (35°/22°) × 0.5 × `neckFollow`, eased at 3/s. Eyes lead, head follows. `FaceTracker` now also keeps `rawX/rawY` (direction-corrected, no gain).
* No face, idle: random wander every 6–15 s. Conversation, no face: hold. Dozing: head low, slow drift. ASLEEP: turned away (0.6 × range) and fully down. THINKING: +8° pan, 6° up. SPEAKING: bob with voice. Expressions add gestures: nod (tilt wave), shake_head (pan wave), happy perk up, surprised pull back, sad droop, curious, look_away. Eye nod/shake wobble shrinks when the neck is connected.
* Servo angle = center trim ± pose (`flipPan/flipTilt`); sent as text `M <pan> <tilt>\n` (Locale.US, 1 decimal), only when changed or once a second.
* `NeckLink`: BLE central. Service `4f726269-742d-4e65-636b-000000000001`, write-without-response characteristic `…0002`. Scans with a service-UUID filter (15 s, retries every 5 s), remembers `neckAddress`, then connects directly; reconnects on drop; one write in flight, newest move replaces waiting ones. Permissions: BLUETOOTH_SCAN (neverForLocation) + BLUETOOTH_CONNECT on 12+, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION + legacy BLUETOOTH/ADMIN (maxSdk 30) on older; requested from the Neck tab when switched on.
* Firmware: Arduino, works with esp32 core 2.x and 3.x (`ESP_ARDUINO_VERSION_MAJOR`). LEDC 50 Hz 14-bit on GPIO 18 (pan, 270° servo) and 19 (tilt, 180° servo), 500–2500 µs. Hard limits pan 45–225, tilt 45–135; center 135/90. Easing 0.35 per 20 ms step, max 220°/s. Pan relaxes (no pulses) after 3 s still; tilt holds (carries the phone). Same commands over Serial (115200): `M p t`, `C`, `X` (relax), `?`. Advertises the service UUID, name `Orbit-Neck` in the scan response.

## Settled design decisions

* Phone ↔ servo controller: **Bluetooth** (BLE), not USB. Arm comes later.
* The app talks to a **body interface**. For now a "screen-only body" acts out gestures with the eyes; the Bluetooth body plugs in later without changing the rest.
* AI chooses from a small set of predefined gestures (tools like `nod`, `tilt`, `look\_away`), never raw motor commands.
* Behavior state machine: Asleep → Waking (presence, tap, or wake word) → Talking (only state with an AI session open) ↔ Idle/watching (tracks face) → Glancing around (user left) → Asleep.
* Local cues (user starts or stops talking) trigger listening/thinking poses immediately, without waiting for the AI.
* AI: Gemini Live on the free tier is the leading candidate. Mind session limits (\~15 min audio-only by default) and reconnect handling.
* Vision: face tracking stays local. Photos go to the AI only when the person asks it to look (implemented 2026-10-28 as the `look` tool: on by default, switchable, with a visible "looking" cue in the eyes).
* Character: generic and friendly by default; personality prompt kept in its own file so it's swappable later. It may start conversations, and the user can tell it not to. It should remember the user across days.
* Privacy model: four modes. Attentive (camera tracking + wake-word listening), Dozing (presence check + wake word), Sleep (mic off, on-phone face check 1/s to wake on return), Privacy sleep (camera AND mic fully off; wake by double-tap only; "privacy mode"). Privacy sleep is visibly distinct (darker eyes, status line, neck turns away).
