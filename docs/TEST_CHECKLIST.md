# Orbit test checklist

Tick each one off, and note anything odd. "Say" means during a conversation (tap the eyes,
or say "Orby").

## Eyes
- [ ] Start-up: moon orbits the planet, then both slide smoothly into the eyes (no jump)
- [ ] Say "make a happy face": rings open at the bottom into smiling arches, then close again
- [ ] Move side to side and up/down: eyes follow further than before, never clipped at an edge
- [ ] No dark bar anywhere with scan lines on (try a few line counts on the Eyes tab)
- [ ] Double-tap: eyes shut, breathe slowly, little z's float up; they drift slowly over time
- [ ] Double-tap again: wakes

## Talking
- [ ] Say "what am I holding?" (hold something up): eyes widen, it describes it
- [ ] Say "what's on my desk?": eyes look down; without the neck it says it can't tilt yet
- [ ] Say "remind me in 1 minute to stretch": it confirms the time; a minute later it chimes,
      looks surprised and tells you (try once mid-conversation, once after it has ended)
- [ ] Say "set a 2 minute timer", then "cancel the timer": it cancels
- [ ] Reminders show on the Memory tab with Cancel buttons
- [ ] Double-tap to sleep, set a 1-minute reminder first: it wakes up for it
- [ ] Say "louder" / "quieter"
- [ ] Say "goodnight": says goodnight, then sleeps
- [ ] Say "that's all": says bye, stops listening
- [ ] Say "don't say hi when I come back", then check the greeting switch is off (turn it back on)

## Its own life
- [ ] Memory tab → Do homework now: a hobby and a journal entry appear
- [ ] Say "what have you been up to?": it mentions its hobby
- [ ] Next time it greets you after homework: a quick hello plus one line about what it learned
- [ ] Say "you should take up astronomy": the hobby changes on the Memory tab

## Arriving
- [ ] Leave for 10+ minutes, come back: happy eyes, quick hello
- [ ] (Optional) Conversation tab → "Notice things when I arrive" on: next arrival it may
      mention something it sees (once a day)

## Neck tab (no hardware needed)
- [ ] Moves box: the dot follows you as you move; buttons nod / shake / droop etc.
- [ ] "Show the neck's angles on the eyes": small text appears in the top corner (then turn off)

## Sound & life (new tab)
- [ ] Start-up: a soft whoosh with the orbit animation
- [ ] Preview buttons play each sound; volume slider works; Sounds off silences them
- [ ] Tap to talk: a small rising tone; conversation ends: a falling tone
- [ ] Double-tap: a sigh as it falls asleep; double-tap again: a chirp as it wakes
- [ ] Leave it until it dozes: it yawns (eyes squeeze, little yawn sound)
- [ ] Clap once loudly while it's idle: it jumps (and wakes if dozing)
- [ ] Nobody in view, wave a hand at the side of the camera: the eyes glance toward it
- [ ] While it watches you, the eyes make tiny flicks (subtle)
- [ ] Come back after 10+ min: glance away, double-take, then happy eyes
- [ ] "Set a 3 minute timer": a thin ring appears around the right eye and drains;
      when done, the rings pulse and a rising chime plays
- [ ] Weather in the eyes: enter your town → Show now → Back: tint + rain/snow/etc.
- [ ] Replay its first wake-up: eyes flutter open, look around, then it introduces itself
- [ ] (Later) Gentle nudges: after 2 h at the desk it suggests a stretch; after 11 pm, bed

## Alive, round 2
- [ ] Sound & life → "Try one" buttons: each plays on the eyes (slow blink, content, wonder,
      ponder, sneeze, hiccup, stretch, firefly, roll eyes, peek, shiver)
- [ ] Firefly: a little glowing dot drifts around; the eyes follow it
- [ ] Leave it idle with you there for a few minutes: little moves happen by themselves
- [ ] Talk near it (not to it): the eyes perk up and tilt a little
- [ ] Ask "show me you're content" / "ponder that": it uses the new expressions

## Sleep
- [ ] Double-tap to sleep while sitting there: it stays asleep. Leave for 30 s, come back: it wakes
- [ ] Say "privacy mode": camera and mic off ("camera and microphone off" hint); coming back does
      NOT wake it; double-tap does
- [ ] Sound & life previews: every sound is soft; no "slap" (especially Startle, Sneeze, Hiccup)

## Looking at your screen
- [ ] (Neck needed) Say "Orby, find my screen" (or Neck tab → Find my screen): it looks around for
      ~30 s, turns to the screen, asks if that's right; "a bit left" / "up" / "perfect" work
- [ ] (Optional) "Or aim by hand" still works
- [ ] Ask "what do you think of what's on my screen?" → the head turns to the screen, then back;
      it describes what's there
- [ ] Without a neck: it says it can't turn to the screen yet

## Orbit Desk (optional)
- [ ] On the PC: run_orbit_desk.bat → shows name, address, pairing code
- [ ] Computer tab → Find my computer → pick it → code → Test → "✓ Connected to …"
- [ ] Computer tab → "Optional: sharper text with Orbit Desk" on → ask about your screen → eyes
      glance toward the computer, it describes the screen; the PC window lists the look
- [ ] Type p + Enter on the PC, ask again → it says sharing is paused
- [ ] Eyes tab → Copy my look → change something → Paste a look → it goes back

## Phone care
- [ ] Tracking & night tab shows the phone temperature and "fine"

## Name and icon
- [ ] App shows as "Orbit" with the new icon in the app drawer
